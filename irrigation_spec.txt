Smart Garden Irrigation Controller

The device automates watering for a small home garden using soil moisture
sensing and local weather awareness, running outdoors on solar + battery
power with no wired mains connection.

Requirements:

- Microcontroller: Low-power 32-bit MCU with built-in Wi-Fi, needed to
  check local rain forecast via API and log watering history to a home
  server. Deep sleep current should be minimal since the device wakes only
  briefly every 15 minutes to check sensors.

- Soil moisture sensor: Capacitive (not resistive, to avoid corrosion),
  analog output, suitable for direct burial in soil, low power.

- Water flow sensor: Inline pulse-output flow sensor to measure how much
  water is dispensed per watering cycle, standard 1/2 inch pipe fitting.

- Solenoid valve driver: MOSFET or driver IC capable of switching a
  12V DC solenoid valve from a 3.3V logic signal, with flyback protection
  for the valve's inductive coil.

- Battery and solar: Single-cell 18650 Li-ion battery, charged by a small
  solar panel through a solar-optimized charge controller. Needs a fuel
  gauge or simple voltage divider to estimate remaining battery percentage.

- Voltage regulator: Efficient buck converter or LDO to supply 3.3V logic
  from the battery, minimal quiescent current for long battery life
  between charges.

- Status LED: Single RGB or bi-color LED to indicate system status
  (watering active, low battery, Wi-Fi connected).

- Weatherproof enclosure connector: Cable gland or waterproof connector
  for the soil sensor and valve wiring passing through the enclosure wall.

Cost-sensitive - this is a DIY/hobbyist-tier product, target BOM under
$25 per unit at low volume. Prefer widely-available, well-documented parts
over exotic or hard-to-source components.
